Descriptions

InputsCounter counts your keyboard inputs to help you understand how you use your Mac. See which apps and categories you type in most, explore your weekly trends, and switch input sources automatically when you change apps.

Your words stay yours. InputsCounter records only typing counts, never what you type. Your statistics are stored locally on your Mac.

Key features

  1. Keep track of your daily typing count.
  2. Monitor typing counts for the apps you choose.
  3. Switch the input source automatically for each app.
  4. See your typing activity and trends over the last week.
  5. Explore typing counts across different app categories.
  6. A native interface with Liquid Glass on supported macOS versions.
  7. Available in English, Simplified Chinese, and German.
